About Fintech.AI
Fintech.AI is an India-first financial safety assistant designed to help people recognize scams, take immediate protective steps, and report fraud through official channels in their own language.
Mission
Democratize financial safety for every Indian — from metros to villages — in their own language.
Safety rules
  • Never share OTP/UPI PIN.
  • Never install unknown “support” apps from links.
  • If it’s urgent, call 1930 and report on cybercrime.gov.in.
